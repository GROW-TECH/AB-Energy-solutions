Account & Data Deletion

AB Energy Solutions CRM & Solar Business OS • Transparency & User Control

At AB Energy Solutions, we respect your right to control your personal and professional information. Under Google Play Store Developer Guidelines and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), you can request the permanent removal of your account, authentication credentials, and associated personal records at any time.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

You may initiate an account deletion through any of the three options below:

A Method 1: In-App Deletion (Fastest)

  1. Open the AB Energy Solutions CRM app on your Android device.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Tap on your User Avatar / Name in the top navigation bar.
  4. Click Account Profile & Security.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Request Account Deletion.
  6. Confirm your selection to queue immediate deactivation.

B Method 2: Online Deletion Request Form

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, submit the secure form below. Our Data Protection Team will verify and process your request:

Request Logged Successfully. A confirmation ticket has been generated. Our team will verify your registered contact and purge your personal data within 14 business days.

C Method 3: Direct Email Request

Send an email to our support team at support@xiadot.com with the subject line "Account and Data Deletion Request". Please include your registered mobile number and email.

2. What Data Is Permanently Deleted

  • Authentication Records: Username, password hash, biometric tokens, and active session tokens.
  • User Profile: Name, personal contact details, assigned avatar, and employee profile notes.
  • Call Tracking Records: Phone call duration logs, direction indicators, and call history recorded through the sales tracking bridge.
  • Temporary Field Notes: Unsubmitted drafts, transient check-in caches, and personal preference cookies.

3. What Data Is Retained and Why

Statutory & Regulatory Retention: Certain operational documents cannot be immediately purged due to legal and financial compliance requirements under Indian law:
  • GST Tax Invoices & Payment Receipts: Retained for a statutory period of 6 to 8 years in compliance with the Goods and Services Tax (GST) Act and the Indian Companies Act.
  • Executed Solar EPC Contracts & Warranties: Retained for the active warranty term of the solar system (up to 10-25 years on solar PV modules and inverters) to protect consumer statutory claims and grid interconnection guarantees.
  • DISCOM & CEIG Inspection Clearances: Retained as official statutory records for the connected electrical grid utility.

4. Processing Timeline

  • Acknowledgment: Your deletion request is acknowledged within 48 hours.
  • Account Deactivation: Immediate upon receipt. Your login credentials will cease to function.
  • Data Scrubbing: Personal identifiers and associated call logs are permanently deleted from production and backup clusters within 14 to 30 calendar days.
