Privacy Policy

AB Energy Solutions CRM & Solar Business OS • Effective Date: November 16, 2026

AB Energy Solutions ("we", "our", or "us") operates the AB Energy Solutions CRM mobile application and web portal. We provide turnkey Solar EPC (Engineering, Procurement, Construction) and distributed rooftop solar project management software. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal and technical data in compliance with Google Play Store Developer Policies, the Information Technology Act (2000), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP).

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver solar engineering feasibility assessments, quotation generation, project execution, and client management services:

A. User Account & Profile Information

  • Full Name, Business Email, and Mobile Number: Used for account creation, role-based access control (RBAC), and team coordination (Super Admin, Sales Manager, Sales Executive, Survey Engineer, Project Manager, Purchase Team, Installation Team, Accounts, Auditor).
  • Customer & Client Details: Consumer names, site installation addresses, DISCOM electricity bill amounts, consumer connection numbers, and rooftop specifications.

B. Site Survey, Engineering & Camera Data

  • Rooftop Photographs: Captured via device camera or photo library for assessing shadow clearance, panel fixing orientation, inverter mounting locations, cable raceways, and earth pit soil conditions.
  • Geographic Location Data: Precise or approximate GPS coordinates collected strictly when performing an active rooftop site audit to calculate solar irradiance, azimuth angle, and statutory DISCOM net-metering feasibility.
  • Engineering Documents: Single Line Diagrams (SLD), CEIG inspection approvals, structural wind-load certificates, and PM Surya Ghar subsidy claim receipts.

C. Telephony & Sales Call Logs

  • Call Log Metadata: Call duration (seconds), timestamp, phone number, and call direction (Incoming, Outgoing, Missed).
  • Purpose of Call Tracking: In order to allow solar sales executives to track customer consultation calls, calculate response times, and append notes to the customer's solar project timeline, the application reads call logs upon user authorization.
  • Scope Limitation: We never record call audio, eavesdrop on private conversations, or access contacts not related to assigned CRM leads.

D. Financial & Payment Verification Data

  • Payment milestone receipts, bank transaction references (UTR / Cheque numbers), GST invoice records, and proof-of-payment screenshots uploaded for statutory accounting verification.

2. Android Device Permissions Requested

The AB Energy Solutions CRM Android application requests specific runtime permissions. You are prompted before each permission is utilized:

Permission Tag Category Operational Purpose
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notifications Delivers real-time status alerts for lead assignments, payment receipts, survey schedules, and BOM deliveries.
CAMERA Hardware Enables site engineers to capture rooftop audit photos and accounts staff to snap payment receipts.
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Storage Allows selecting rooftop images, PDF single-line diagrams, and UTR receipts from device storage.
READ_CALL_LOG / READ_PHONE_STATE Telephony Enables sales reps to auto-log consultation call duration directly into the customer CRM timeline.
CALL_PHONE Telephony Permits 1-tap Click-to-Call from client lead cards to the native phone dialer.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Location Enables accurate solar radiation mapping and geo-tagging of rooftop survey audits.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Solar System Sizing: Generating automated 3 kW to 100 kW solar engineering quotes, BOM indents, and estimated electricity bill savings.
  • Statutory Approvals: Preparing DISCOM net-metering feasibility filings, CEIG safety clearances, and PM Surya Ghar National Portal central subsidy documentation.
  • Field Operations: Coordinating warehouse inventory dispatch, installation crew check-ins, and milestone commissioning inspections.
  • Customer Accounting: Generating GST-compliant advance and commissioning invoices with verifiable UTR payment receipts.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal or operational data to any third party or marketing broker. Data is shared only under strict operational necessity:

  • Statutory & Government Bodies: DISCOM electricity utilities (e.g. BESCOM, TANGEDCO), state electrical inspectorates (CEIG), and national subsidy portals for net-metering sanction letters.
  • Authorized Internal Personnel: Role-based access ensures only assigned managers, engineers, or executives can review specific lead or customer records.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by court order, law enforcement, or statutory audit requirements.

5. Data Retention and Security

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, salted password hashing, and encrypted MySQL cloud databases.

Data is retained for as long as your organizational account remains active, or as required by applicable Indian tax and electrical regulations (typically 5 to 7 years for GST tax invoices and commissioning sign-off records).
